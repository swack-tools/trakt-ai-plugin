Your controls

  • Limit access: connect with trakt:read for a connection that cannot make changes.
  • Sign out: DELETE /auth/session revokes access at Trakt and deletes your records, as described below.
  • Revoke at Trakt: remove the app from your Trakt account settings at any time.

  • Stop using it: records are deleted automatically 30 days after last use.

What passes through the service

Your client sends tool arguments such as a title query, media type, filter, item identifier, or requested write body and a connection credential. The service sends the needed API requests to Trakt and returns metadata, account records, or operation results to your client. Authorized writes can change lists, watchlists, watched history, ratings, collections, or other supported Trakt resources. The assistant receives the result in your chosen client. The plugin does not need your private files or unrelated conversation history.

Trakt handles your password and approval on its own website. The service receives Trakt access and refresh credentials after approval; those credentials are not returned to the assistant. Device codes are temporary authorization material and should not be posted publicly.

Storage and isolation

Trakt tokens are stored encrypted with a key held only by your AI clients; records are deleted 30 days after last use or on sign-out. It is decrypted in memory only while handling a request.

Each Trakt account has one authorization, encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a random 256-bit connection key. The service does not store that key. Your AI clients hold it sealed inside their plugin credentials, which they cannot read; the service unseals it with a deployment secret for the length of each request. A copy of the stored data alone cannot decrypt a Trakt token, but someone holding both the deployment secret and a client's credential could. The first browser login shows the key once so you can save it. It connects another AI client without another Trakt login, so treat it like a password. A new Trakt login replaces the authorization and invalidates the old key, which disconnects your other clients; the login page warns you before it does.

Durable Objects also hold hashed plugin credentials, each connection's permission, and rate-limit counters keyed by a salted hash of your network address. During login, new Trakt tokens can wait for up to 15 minutes sealed under the deployment secret instead of your key, so a temporary failure does not waste your approval.

Reads fetch Trakt records, and requested writes update Trakt. The service does not keep a separate viewing-history database or local library copy. Your AI client may retain returned results in its conversation history under that provider’s policies.

Permission and changes

Browser OAuth consent identifies the requested read and write scopes. A read-only grant cannot call write operations; refresh preserves its scope. A write also needs explicit user intent, represented by confirmed: true. This control does not promise that every mistaken change can be undone. After an uncertain failure, inspect the account state before repeating a mutation.

Retention and sign-out

The Trakt authorization and connection records are deleted automatically 30 days after their last use. Rate-limit counters and abandoned logins are deleted within an hour. Client registrations hold only a client's name and redirect addresses, and they stay until a future cleanup or a deployment that removes them.

Authenticated DELETE /auth/session signs you out: it asks Trakt to revoke access, then deletes the authorization and the records of every client connected to that Trakt account, because they share it. If Trakt does not confirm the revocation, for example because it is rate limiting the service, your records are still deleted and the response says so. Revoke the app in your Trakt account settings in that case.

The automatic 30-day deletion cannot revoke access at Trakt, because the service does not hold your key at that point. The token stays valid at Trakt until it expires or you revoke the app there. Uninstalling the plugin or disconnecting a client does not sign you out, and neither erases client conversation history. Provider backups and operational retention are outside the app’s deletion code and are not represented as instantly erased.

If you have lost access to a connection, email support@swacktech.com to request deletion. Do not send credentials or post personal history in a public issue. Use security@swacktech.com for security-sensitive communication.

Logs and third parties

App code does not intentionally log request bodies or credentials. Cloudflare observability is enabled and traces are sampled; the hosting provider can process network addresses, request metadata, and operational logs. Cloudflare’s Workers Logs documentation describes how long those logs are retained; this app makes no no-logging guarantee. Trakt and the AI client independently process the data sent to them.

Read the Cloudflare privacy policy, Trakt privacy policy, and your AI client’s privacy settings and policy. This page describes the app’s behavior; those policies govern what each provider does.
