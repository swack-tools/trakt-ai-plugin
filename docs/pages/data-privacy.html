This community service connects an AI client to the Trakt API. Its publisher is SwackTech LLC, as identified by the owner, and it is not affiliated with Trakt, Anthropic, or OpenAI.

What passes through the service

Your client sends tool arguments such as a title query, media type, genre, or year filter and a connection credential. The service sends the needed API requests to Trakt and returns search results, watched summaries or events, or recommendations to your client. The plugin does not need your private files or unrelated conversation history.

Trakt handles your password and device-code approval on its own website. The service receives Trakt access and refresh credentials after approval; those credentials are not returned to the assistant. Device codes are temporary authorization material and should not be posted publicly.

Storage and isolation

Each authorized connection has a separate Cloudflare Durable Object. It holds upstream tokens, hashed plugin credentials, OAuth state, and rate-limit state. A Cloudflare KV namespace also holds a best-effort copy of the upstream tokens with a 30-day TTL. The application does not add its own encryption layer to token values; storage security and encryption at rest are managed by Cloudflare.

Watched and recommendation results are fetched from Trakt rather than kept as a separate viewing-history database. Your AI client may retain returned results in its conversation history under that provider’s policies.

Expiration is not deletion

Access and refresh credentials expire, but Durable Object records do not currently have an automatic deletion schedule. The KV TTL does not delete the authoritative Durable Object. Do not assume that uninstalling the plugin, disconnecting the client, or waiting for token expiry erases server-side data.

Authenticated DELETE /auth/session deletes that connection’s application storage and its KV token copy. Each separate connection must be deleted separately. It does not erase client conversation history or revoke the upstream Trakt grant; revoke the application in Trakt account settings as well. Provider backups and operational retention are outside the application’s deletion code and are not represented as instantly erased.

If you have lost access to a connection, use support to request a safe recovery/deletion process. Do not post credentials or personal history in a public issue. Use security@swacktech.com for security-sensitive communication. The operator’s retention policy and legal notice still require final owner review before directory submission.

Logs and third parties

Application code does not intentionally log request bodies or credentials. Cloudflare observability is enabled and traces are sampled; the hosting provider can process network addresses, request metadata, and operational logs. No unverified retention duration or no-logging guarantee is made. Trakt and the AI client independently process the data sent to them.

Read the Cloudflare privacy policy, Trakt privacy policy, and your AI client’s privacy settings and policy. This page documents the application’s behavior; it does not replace those policies or an owner-reviewed privacy notice.
